DEMO BANK UZ
CUSTOMER RECORDS AND INTERNAL CONTROL POLICY

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT — NOT FOR OPERATIONAL USE

Document version: 1.0
Revision date: 10 October 2026
Organization: Demo Bank UZ, a fictional commercial bank
Responsible departments: Compliance and Customer Operations

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy describes customer identification, customer-record retention,
access to records, and selected internal review controls at Demo Bank UZ.
It applies to retail accounts opened through branches and digital channels.
It does not cover corporate customers, correspondent banking, or transaction
monitoring procedures.

2. CUSTOMER IDENTIFICATION

Before activating an account, Customer Operations must obtain the customer's
full name, date of birth, residential address, and an approved identity-document
number. Staff must compare the customer with the identity document and record
the method and date of verification.

For digital onboarding, the customer must complete a live video check. A copy
of the identity document is retained in the customer file. If the image is
unclear, the account must not be activated until a staff member has reviewed
the document.

3. CUSTOMER RECORDS

Customer Operations stores customer-identification records in the bank's
restricted customer-document system. Access is limited to staff whose assigned
work requires the records. Staff must not download customer files to personal
devices or send them through personal email.

The system records the staff account and time when a customer file is opened.
The Compliance team reviews the access log once each calendar year. A manager
may request an additional review after a suspected unauthorized access event.

Customer-identification records are retained for three years after an account
is closed. At the end of that period, Customer Operations may delete the
records after receiving written approval from its department manager.

4. CUSTOMER RISK REVIEW

At onboarding, the Customer Operations officer assigns a low, medium, or high
customer-risk category using the customer's country of residence and expected
account activity. A high-risk classification requires approval from a
Compliance manager before the account is activated.

This policy does not specify a schedule for repeating the customer-risk review
after onboarding. Staff may request a review when they notice a material change
in customer information.

5. RECORD REQUESTS

When Compliance receives an internal request for a customer-identification
record, the team should provide the record promptly. This policy does not set a
deadline for responding to requests from external authorities or specify who
must approve disclosure outside the bank.

6. RESPONSIBILITIES AND EXCEPTIONS

The Head of Customer Operations is responsible for staff training on this
policy. The Compliance team owns the annual access-log review. Exceptions to
the digital video-check procedure may be approved by a Customer Operations
manager when the customer cannot complete the check; the manager must record
the reason in the customer file.

7. POLICY REVIEW

The Compliance team reviews this policy every two years. The policy owner may
also request an earlier review after a significant change to the bank's
products or internal processes. This policy does not describe a process for
monitoring changes in external laws or regulations.
